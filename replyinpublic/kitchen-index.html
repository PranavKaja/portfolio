HOW TO READ IT

Overheard pulled comments from the brands' Instagram posts, tagged them with Gemini and gave the ones aimed at a brand a 0 to 100 response priority score. This is a dated snapshot. Threads change, and the scraper couldn't see every brand reply, so open the thread before you judge a brand by it. The rest of the story is in the case study.
